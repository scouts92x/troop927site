The New River Gorge · West Virginia

What is the Summit Kayak Adventure?

It's a multi-day river voyage down the legendary New River — one of the oldest rivers in the world. Our crew paddles through Class I–IV whitewater, camps on the riverbank, and explores the wild, history-soaked New River Gorge from the water up.

🌊

Real Whitewater

Paddle and raft through rapids that build from splashy riffles to roaring Class IV drops — with trained guides teaching you to read and run the river.

🏕️

Live on the River

Camp on sandy riverbanks under the stars, cook riverside, and fall asleep to the sound of rushing water deep in the gorge.

🧭

Explore the Gorge

Swim the calm pools, fish for smallmouth bass, hike to overlooks, and pass beneath the iconic New River Gorge Bridge — one of the highest in North America.
