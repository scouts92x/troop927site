Pack Smart, Stay Dry

Packing List

On a river trip, everything gets wet unless you keep it dry. Pack light, pack smart, and label it all.

Three Golden Rules

How to Pack for a River

💧

Everything Gets Wet

If it can't get soaked, it goes in a dry bag. Your sleep gear and dry clothes are what you protect most.

🧶

No Cotton on the Water

Cotton stays wet and cold. Wear quick-dry synthetics — nylon, polyester, or a swimsuit — while paddling.

🏷️

Label Everything

Put your name on every item. Pack light — you carry your own gear, and less stuff means less to lose on the river.

🎒 The Big Three Bags

1) A large dry bag for camp clothes, sleeping bag, and anything that must stay dry. 2) A small day dry bag for on-water essentials (sunscreen, snacks, meds). 3) A duffel for the drive and base. The Summit provides the boats, paddles, PFDs, and helmets — you bring the personal gear below.

Worn While Paddling

On-the-Water Kit

This is what you actually wear and reach for in the boat. Quick-dry only.

👕

What You Wear

  • Swimsuit or quick-dry shorts × 2
  • Quick-dry / rash-guard shirts (synthetic) × 2–3 — no cotton
  • Lightweight synthetic layer for cool/rainy paddling
  • Wide-brim or ball cap with a retainer strap
  • Sunglasses with a floating strap (croakies)
👟

Footwear (Must Strap On)

  • River shoes or sport sandals with heel straps (e.g. closed-toe water shoes)
  • No flip-flops or loose slides — they wash away
  • Old sneakers that can get wet work too
  • Neoprene socks (optional, for cool water)

In Your Small Day Dry Bag

  • Reef-safe sunscreen, SPF 30+ — 1 bottle
  • Lip balm with SPF
  • Reusable water bottle (1 L) with a clip or leash
  • Trail snacks / energy bars for the paddling day
  • Any daytime & emergency medication (tell the guides)
  • Small quick-dry towel or bandana
Keep These Dry

Camp & Sleep Gear

Everything here lives in your large dry bag so you have warm, dry clothes and a cozy bed every night on the riverbank.

🛌

Sleep System

  • Sleeping bag (summer weight, ~40–50°F) in a compression sack
  • Sleeping pad — closed-cell foam or inflatable
  • Small camp pillow or stuff-sack pillow (optional)
  • Dedicated dry sleep clothes — worn only in the bag
👖

Dry Camp Clothes

  • Camp shirt & shorts for the evening
  • Warm layer — fleece or hoodie for cool nights
  • Long pants (bugs & cool evenings)
  • Underwear × 3–4 and socks × 3–4
  • Dry camp shoes (light sneakers or sandals)
  • Rain jacket — waterproof, hooded
🧴

Toiletries & Health

  • Toothbrush & travel toothpaste
  • Biodegradable camp soap & small towel
  • Insect repellent (non-aerosol)
  • Personal medications in original bottles
  • Hand sanitizer & any personal hygiene items
  • Quick-dry travel towel
🔦

Handy Extras

  • Headlamp or flashlight + spare batteries
  • Refillable water bottle #2 for camp
  • Small pocket knife (if troop-approved)
  • Waterproof phone/camera case (optional)
  • Zip-lock bags for wet/dirty items
  • Notebook & pen, deck of cards

Paperwork & Money

  • Completed BSA Annual Health & Medical Record (parts A, B & C)
  • Any medication instructions handed to the trip leaders
  • A little cash for the trading post / travel stops
Leave It Home

Do NOT Bring

Less is more on a river. These either get ruined, lost, or aren't allowed.

🚫
  • Cotton clothing for the water (stays cold and wet)
  • Flip-flops or any shoe without a heel strap
  • Valuables, jewelry, or anything you'd hate to lose
  • Glass containers of any kind
🚫
  • Speakers, drones, or video-game devices
  • Sheath / fixed-blade knives
  • Your own boat, paddle, or PFD (the Summit provides them)
  • Aerosol cans (sunscreen sprays, sprays, etc.)

📋 Final Check

The Summit Bechtel Reserve provides your kayak/raft, paddle, PFD, and helmet, plus crew cooking gear and most food. Confirm the official packing list and health requirements with the trip leaders before you buy anything — then pack it dry, label it, and get excited.
