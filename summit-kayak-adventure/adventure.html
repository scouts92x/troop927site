Read the River

The Adventure

Seven days, one legendary river, and every kind of adventure the New River Gorge can throw at a crew.

On the Water & Off

What You'll Do

This isn't a lazy float. It's a full-on river expedition — paddling, camping, swimming, fishing, and exploring a gorge carved over millions of years.

🌊

Run the Whitewater

The New River builds from playful riffles to booming rapids. You'll learn to paddle as a team, read the current, punch through waves, and celebrate at the bottom of every drop. Guides ride with the crew the whole way.

🏕️

Camp on the Riverbank

Pull the boats up on a sandbar, pitch camp, and cook dinner as the gorge glows at sunset. Nights on the river are quiet, wild, and packed with stars.

🎣

Fish & Swim

The New River is famous for smallmouth bass. Between rapids, cast a line, cool off in a calm pool, or just float and soak in the scenery.

🥾

Hike & Explore

Stretch your legs on trails to gorge overlooks, poke around the remnants of old coal-town history, and paddle beneath the famous New River Gorge Bridge.

🦅

Meet the Wild

Bald eagles, herons, deer, river otters, and dense Appalachian forest — the gorge is a living national park, and you'll see it from the best seat in the house.

Know Before You Go

Reading the Rapids

Whitewater is rated on a difficulty scale. On the New River you'll paddle Class I through IV — here's what that means.

IEasy · gentle riffles
IINovice · small waves
IIIIntermediate · big waves
IVAdvanced · powerful drops

🛟 Safety First, Always

Every paddler wears a properly fitted PFD (life jacket) and helmet on the water. Trained river guides lead the crew, brief every rapid, and set safety on the big ones. You never run whitewater alone — the crew runs it together.

June 6–12, 2027

The Flow of the Week

A sample rhythm for our seven days. The base confirms the exact daily plan — but the adventure looks a lot like this.

6SUN

Arrive & Gear Up

Roll into the Summit, meet the guides, get fitted for PFDs and paddles, and learn the safety basics before the first launch.

7MON

Put In

Push off onto the New River. Warm up on the gentle stuff, practice paddle commands, and make our first riverside camp.

8TUE

Build the Skills

Bigger water. Learn to read currents, ferry across the river, and work as a paddle team through Class II–III rapids.

9WED

Rapids & Fishing

Run rapids in the morning, then slow down to fish for smallmouth bass and swim the calm pools in the afternoon.

10THU

The Big Water

The heart of the trip — the crew takes on the New's biggest Class III–IV rapids beneath the towering gorge walls.

11FRI

Explore the Gorge

Hike to an overlook, explore coal-country history, and paddle under the legendary New River Gorge Bridge.

12SAT

Take Out & Head Home

One last stretch of river, a final camp breakdown, and the ride home as a crew that ran a legendary river together.
