Welcome to Troop 927

Adventure, growth, and connection await

Our troop offers a dynamic program designed to help girls build confidence, learn new skills, and make a positive impact on their community. If your daughter is ready for adventure, growth, and connection, Scouting America is the perfect place for her.

What awaits her

What Awaits Her in Scouting America?

We provide a supportive and inspiring environment where girls can explore their interests, develop leadership qualities, and discover their inner strength.

Regular Meetings

Join us every Monday evening at 6:30 PM at St. John's United Methodist Church, 321 S. Oakland Ave, Rock Hill, SC.

Exciting Activities & Adventures

From creative projects and STEM challenges to outdoor explorations and community service, our program is packed with engaging activities that spark curiosity and foster personal growth.

Skill-Building & Personal Growth

Girls learn practical and life skills, develop problem-solving abilities, and gain confidence as they earn badges and complete fun, challenging activities and adventures.

Lasting Friendships

She'll connect with a supportive group of peers, building strong bonds and creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Scouting America is more than just an after-school activity; it's a foundation for success, fostering resilience, compassion, and a can-do attitude.
