Pack Light, Ride Right

Gear & Packing

On a Cavalcade, your horse and the pack horses carry everything — so it all has to fit the saddle bags and stuff sacks.

Good News

Philmont Provides a Lot

You don't haul horse tack or most crew gear. Philmont issues the heavy stuff — you bring personal items, a few specifics, and your own stove.

🐎

All Horse Gear

Saddles, bridles, halters, saddle bags, and helmets — all provided and fitted at the ranch.

⛺

Crew Camp Gear

Tents, pots, dining fly, bear bags & rope, camp suds, matches, toilet paper, and a packing stuff sack.

🔥

You Bring the Stove

Cavalcade crews must bring their own backpacking stoves (or ship them ahead) — this is on us.

📏 Everything Must Fit

Personal gear packs into a stuff sack (about 21¼" long × 11" diameter) and saddle bags (~3" × 12" × 12" per side). Larger sacks aren't permitted. Philmont's stuff sack is not waterproof — bring your own 30L waterproof stuff sack (same dimensions) if you want one. You may not bring your own saddle, bridle, saddle pad, or saddle bags.

The Packing List

What Goes Where

Organized the way it packs onto the horses — aligned with Philmont's official "What to Bring · For Cavalcade" equipment list.

T = Tooth of Time Traders A = Accessible on horseback BB = Bear bag at night S = Share with a buddy O = Optional E = Philmont Essential * = Western riding boots required

👢 On Your Body While Riding (Philmont Essentials)

Western riding boots (smooth-soled, ~1" heel — no lug soles or laced boots), western-cut jeans (baggy jeans wrinkle and chafe), a long-sleeve shirt tucked in, a belt, and the Philmont-provided helmet. Bring rain pants one size larger than usual so you can mount and ride freely.

🧥

Tied to Your Saddle

  • Rain jacket — sturdy, waterproof, hooded (T, A)
  • Rain pants — waterproof (T, A)
  • Warm jacket — wool or fleece (T, A)
🐴

Saddle Bags

  • 2 × one-quart plastic water bottles with secure lids — no CamelBaks / bladders (T, A, BB)
🍎

Feed Bag

  • Morning/lunch food (provided) (BB)
  • Daytime & emergency medication (BB)
  • Lip balm SPF 25+ & sunscreen (2–3, 6 oz, SPF 25+) (T, BB)
  • Camera & Philmont map (T, A, S)
🛌

Personal Stuff Sack — Sleep & Clothing

  • Sleeping bag — rated 20°F, under 5 lb, in a compression sack (T)
  • Waterproof stuff sack / 4–6 mil plastic liner (T)
  • Sleeping pad — closed-cell foam or inflatable (T)
  • Sleep clothes — worn in bag only (tee & gym shorts) (T)
  • Underwear × 3, hiking shorts, sports bra as needed (T)
  • Short-sleeve shirt — wicking, no cotton or nylon (T)
  • Hiking boots/shoes for camp & side hikes — ankle support recommended (T)
  • Hiking/boot socks × 3 — synthetic or wool (T)
  • Uniform shirt & pants — for travel (O)
🧭

Personal Stuff Sack — Miscellaneous

  • Baseball cap or wide-brim hat (T)
  • Pocket knife — small, no sheath knives (T, A, S)
  • Matches/lighter in a waterproof container (T, A, BB, S)
  • Flashlight/headlamp + extra batteries (T)
  • Compass — liquid-filled, rotating dial (T, A, S)
  • Bandana or Buff × 2 (T, BB)
  • Biodegradable soap & small camp towel (T, BB, S)
  • Sunglasses, watch, whistle, notepad & pen (T)
  • $50 in small bills (BB) · 2–3 small stuff sacks & ditty bags (T)
👝

Crew Stuff Sack (Crew of 10–15)

  • Mess kit — deep bowl, mug/cup (12–20 oz), spoon/spork (T)
  • Toiletries, toothbrush & toothpaste, comb, tampons/pads (T, BB, S)
  • Shaving cream (no aerosol), sewing kit (T, BB, S)
  • Tent stakes — 5 per person (8 per tent + 8 for dining fly) (T)
  • Weight-bearing (climbing-rated, locking) carabiner (T)
  • 2.5-gal collapsible water containers × 2–3 (T)
  • Fuel funnel, duct tape, cooking spices (T, BB)
  • Nylon cord × 3 (50' × 1/8") (T)
  • Insect repellent × 2 (no aerosol) & water purifiers/filters (T, BB, O)
🐎

Pannier Bags / Pack Horses

  • Crew first aid kit (T, BB)
  • 2.5-gal collapsible water jugs × 2 (crew provides) (T)
  • Backpacking stoves × 2–3 — white gas or isobutane (T)
  • Fuel bottles × 2 (resupply at commissary camps) (T)
  • Waterproof ground cloth — 6' × 8', one per tent (T)
🎁

Provided by Philmont (Free)

  • Nylon dining fly (12'×10') + collapsible poles
  • Philmont tent — 1 per 2 campers
  • Cooking pot (8 qt), dishwashing pot, beverage pot
  • Chef cutlery kit & hot-pot tongs
  • Bear rope (100'×1/4") × 2 & bear bags (3–6)
  • Strainer & scraper, trash bags, salt & pepper
  • Micropur water-purifier tablets & toilet paper (resupplied)
  • All horse tack: saddles, bridles, halters, saddle bags, helmets
🚫

Items NOT Allowed

  • Deodorant (attracts bears)
  • Radios / MP3 players / video game devices
  • Hammocks & video cameras
  • Drones
  • Turkey bags
  • Bear bag pulley systems, bear spray, bear horns
  • Your own saddle, bridle, saddle pad, or saddle bags
