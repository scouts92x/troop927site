What is a Philmont Cavalcade?

A Cavalcade is Philmont's horseback high-adventure trek. Instead of backpacking, our crew spends six of eight days on horseback, covering the same legendary Sangre de Cristo backcountry the old miners, cowboys, and mountain men once rode.

🐴

Your Own Horse

Each rider is matched with a horse for the whole trek. A Philmont Horseman & Wrangler ride with the crew, teach horsemanship, and care for the string.

⛰️

Legendary Terrain

Trails climb from 6,500 to 11,000 feet through pine forest, meadows, and canyons — with the iconic Tooth of Time watching over it all.

🤠

Old-West Program

Chuck-wagon dinners, gold panning, blacksmithing, campfires, and a final-day Gymkhana — light-hearted riding games to cap the ride.
