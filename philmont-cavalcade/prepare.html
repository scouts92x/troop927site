Physical Conditioning

Build Your Trail Fitness

Cavalcade riding is strenuous. You'll be in the saddle 3–8+ hours a day, plus side hikes on layover days. Regular aerobic exercise is highly recommended.

  • Exercise 30–60 minutes, 3–5 days a week. Consistency beats intensity — start slow and build.
  • Best activities: jogging, running or hiking uphill, long flights of stairs, and hiking with a full pack.
  • Cross-train: swimming, cycling, stationary bike, and aerobic classes all help.
  • Begin 6+ months out. Philmont recommends starting a regular program well before arrival — the earlier in the 2028 plan, the better.
  • Strengthen your legs & core. Sitting balanced in the saddle for eight hours takes real stamina.
  • Watch the scale. Riders must be at or under 200 lb — build conditioning and healthy habits into the plan now.
In the Saddle

Learn the Horse

There's a huge difference between eight hours on a Philmont trail and an hour at the local stable. Come with a positive attitude, a willingness to learn, and respect for the animals.

🏅

Earn Horsemanship MB

Philmont highly recommends every rider complete the Horsemanship Merit Badge. It's the single best way to prepare for the trek.

🪢

Practice Riding & Saddling

Get real saddle time. After instruction at the ranch, each rider saddles and unsaddles their own horse and helps pack the string.

Thin Air

Respect the Altitude

Philmont sits above 6,500 ft — all of it qualifies for Acute Mountain Sickness. There's 20–35% less oxygen than at sea level, and teens have a higher incidence of AMS.

  • Hydrate hard. Plan on 6–8 quarts of water a day. You lose fluids even when you don't feel thirsty.
  • Eat full meals & sleep well the first days at elevation to help your body adjust.
  • Know the AMS signs: headache, insomnia, fatigue, shortness of breath, nausea, irritability.
  • Arrive rested. A good night's sleep after the opening campfire helps you acclimate before the ride.
Don't Get Turned Away

Paperwork & Shakedowns

🩺

Health Record Timing

The AHMR physical is valid for 12 months — schedule it so it's still current in June 2028. Confirm your tetanus is within 10 years, keep blood pressure under 140/90, and pack all emergency meds in original containers.

⛺

Crew Shakedowns

Good camping and cooking skills are still essential on a Cavalcade. We'll run practice campouts to sharpen cooking, camp setup, bear-bag hangs, and teamwork — the Patrol Method in action.

🌦️ Prepare for Every Weather

New Mexico high country can hit 90°F+ by day and drop to freezing at night, with sudden afternoon rain, hail, or even summer snow. Durable rain gear (jacket and pants) and warm layers aren't optional — pack for all of it.
